EMR integration and patient portals

Connect your EMR's patient portal to your website, so patients find forms, appointments and refills in one place.

In plain words

A patient portal is the secure login area that comes with your electronic medical record (EMR) system, where patients can fill out forms, request appointments, ask for refills and see results. This service connects that portal to your practice website, so patients start in one familiar place and are sent to the right portal page for each task. Without that connection, most patients never find the portal and call the front desk instead.

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Portal access on your website

A clearly labeled patient portal button in your site's menu and on key pages, so patients reach the login in one click.

Patient resources page

One page that gathers forms, appointment requests, refill requests and results, each with a short explanation and a direct link to the portal.

Online forms connection

Links to your EMR's intake and consent forms, so new patients complete paperwork at home and answers arrive in their chart.

Appointment request path

A booking link or embedded scheduler from your EMR, placed where patients look for it, including service pages and the contact page.

Patient how-to instructions

Short, plain instructions on creating an account, resetting a password and requesting a refill, written to reduce phone calls to your staff.

Integration summary document

A written record of every link, page and setting we added, so your staff and EMR vendor know exactly how things connect.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Discovery call

    We ask which EMR you use, which portal features are turned on and what patients call about most often. You provide a contact at the practice who knows the EMR and the name of your compliance contact.

  2. Integration plan

    We map which portal features will appear on the website and where, based on what your EMR allows. You review the plan and confirm with your EMR vendor any features that need to be switched on.

  3. Build and test

    We add the links, pages and instructions, then test each path on phones and computers using a test account or a walkthrough with your staff. Patient information stays inside the portal, and the setup is done with HIPAA requirements in mind and reviewed with your compliance contact.

  4. Launch and upkeep

    After your approval we publish the changes and give your front desk a short guide for pointing patients to the website. We check the links periodically and update them when your EMR vendor changes its portal, and you tell us when you add or remove features.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

EMR patient portal

The secure patient login area supplied by your EMR vendor, where records, messages and requests are kept. We do not rebuild or replace it, we connect your website to it using the links and options your vendor provides.

Content management system

The software that runs your website and lets pages be edited without programming, such as WordPress. We use it to add the portal buttons, the resources page and the patient instructions.

Embedded scheduling widget

A small booking window supplied by many EMR vendors that can sit inside a web page. Where your EMR offers one, we place it on your site so patients request a time without hunting for the right screen.

Website analytics

A tool that counts visits and clicks on your website without identifying individual patients. We use it to see how often the portal links are used and which pages need clearer directions, and we keep tracking limited to the public website.

Words you will hear

Single sign-on
A setup that lets a person use one username and password to reach more than one system, which some EMR vendors offer and some do not.
API
A way for two software systems to exchange information automatically, which some EMR vendors make available to connect scheduling or forms to a website.
Protected health information (PHI)
Any information that can identify a patient and relates to their health, care or payment, which HIPAA requires practices and their vendors to safeguard.

  • Your front desk spends much of the day on refill requests, scheduling calls and paper forms
  • You are launching or redesigning your website and want the portal built into it from the start

Questions we hear

How does connecting our EMR's patient portal to our website actually work?

Your EMR vendor already provides the online patient portal, and we connect it to your medical website as part of an overall integration plan. Patients then reach forms, appointments and prescription refills from one place on your site instead of hunting for a separate login link. The medical records themselves stay inside your EMR's portal, and your website serves as the front door to it.

What do we need to provide to get started?

We need to know which EMR you use, whether its patient portal is already active, and which features you want patients to reach from the site, such as online forms, appointments or refill requests. It also helps to have a contact at your office or at your EMR vendor who can confirm the portal settings and links. To go over the details, you can call us at 954-358-2856.

How long does the integration take and what does it cost?

Both depend on your practice, mainly on which EMR you use, what its portal allows and how your current website is built. We provide a quote and a plan after a free analysis of your setup, so you know the scope before any work begins.

What should we watch out for before linking the portal to our site?

The features available to patients depend on what your EMR's portal supports, so it is worth confirming with your vendor which functions are turned on, such as lab results, online forms or appointment requests. Your staff should also be ready to handle the requests that come in through the portal, because a refill or appointment request that sits unanswered frustrates patients. We recommend placing the portal link where patients can find it easily on every page, since a portal that is hard to find tends to go unused.
