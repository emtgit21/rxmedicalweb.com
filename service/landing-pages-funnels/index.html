Landing pages and patient funnels

One page, one treatment, one action. Landing pages built for a single campaign convert far better than sending ad traffic to your home page.

When a patient clicks an ad for a specific treatment, they should land on a page about that treatment, with the answer to their question and an easy way to book. That is a landing page.

What we build

  • A page for each treatment, promotion or event
  • Offers and forms designed around one clear action
  • Before-and-after galleries, FAQs and physician credentials
  • Instant follow-up by text and email when a patient submits
  • A/B tests of headlines, offers and layouts
  • Tracking from the first click to the booked consultation

AI tools let us produce and test variations quickly. Our physician advisors review the medical claims on every page.
