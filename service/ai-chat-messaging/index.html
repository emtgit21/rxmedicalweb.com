AI agents for your practice

Assistants on your website, your text line and your phone that answer patients at any hour, take booking requests and hand off to your staff.

A large share of calls to a practice arrive when nobody can pick up: at lunch, after hours, or while the front desk is with a patient. Every one of those is a patient who may call the next practice on the list.

We set up AI agents trained on your services, hours, locations and policies, so patients get an answer right away.

What we set up

  • Website chat that answers questions about your services and takes booking requests
  • Text-back for missed calls, so a caller hears from you in seconds
  • Voice agent that answers the phone after hours, in English and Spanish
  • Reminders and follow-ups by text
  • Hand-off to your staff, with the conversation attached, whenever a person is needed
  • Answers limited to what you approve, and reports on what patients ask most

Agents for your team, too

  • Call summaries, so your manager knows what every caller wanted
  • Draft replies to reviews, emails and social messages for your approval
  • A private assistant trained on your own policies and price list for new staff

The agents do not give medical advice or diagnose. Clinical questions go to your team, and the setup is done with HIPAA requirements in mind.
