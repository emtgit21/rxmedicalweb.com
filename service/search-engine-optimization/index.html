Search engine optimization for doctors

SEO gets your practice website to appear high in the results when patients search for the procedures you offer.

In plain words

Search engine optimization, or SEO, is the ongoing work of making your practice website appear near the top of the results when patients search for the procedures you offer. Patients tend to choose from the first few results they see, so a site that ranks well brings in visitors without a fee for every click. It is not a one-time setup, so our staff works on your website daily.

This is a good fit if

  • You offer procedures patients actively search for and your website does not appear near the top of the results
  • You are paying for every click on ads and want a steadier, more cost effective source of website visitors
  • Your practice is willing to invest in work that builds gradually and holds its value with ongoing maintenance

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Keyword list and strategy

A researched list of the words patients type when looking for your procedures, with a plan for which pages should target each one.

Reorganized site structure

Your pages and menus arranged so that both patients and search engines can find every procedure you offer in a click or two.

Search-ready page code

Titles, descriptions, image labels and structured data added to every page, so search engines can read what each page covers.

Physician-reviewed content

New and improved pages about your procedures, written or reviewed by physicians on our staff so the information is accurate and useful.

Ethical link building

Relevant websites linking to yours, earned through honest methods only, which signals to search engines that your site can be trusted.

Custom infographics

Illustrated summaries of a procedure or topic that patients find easy to read and that other websites like to share and link to.

How it works

  1. Learn your practice

    We talk with you about your procedures, your location and the patients you most want to reach. You tell us your priorities and give us access to your current website.

  2. Research and plan

    We study how competing practices in your area rank and build your keyword list and content plan. You review the list and confirm it matches the procedures you want to promote.

  3. Optimize the site

    We adjust the structure, set up the code on every page and write or revise the content. You or a designated clinician approve the medical content before it is published.

  4. Daily ongoing work

    We keep adding content, earning links and watching how your pages rank, adjusting as search engines and competitors change. You let us know about new procedures, providers or locations so the site stays current.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Google Search

The search engine most patients use to find a doctor or research a procedure. It is the main place we work to have your pages appear, though the same work helps on other search engines too.

Structured data

A small block of code, invisible to visitors, that tells search engines plain facts such as your specialty, address and hours. We add it to every page so your practice is described correctly in the results.

Keyword research tools

Software that shows which words people search for and how many other websites compete for them. We use it to choose terms that real patients use and that your practice has a realistic chance of ranking for.

AI language models

Software that can draft and analyze text quickly. We use it to assist with research and first drafts, and our staff and physicians review everything before it goes on your site.

Words you will hear

Organic results
The unpaid listings on a search results page, which a search engine orders by how relevant and trustworthy it judges each website to be.
Title tag
The page name written in a website's code, which appears as the clickable headline of your listing in the search results.
White hat SEO
Optimization methods that follow the search engines' published rules, as opposed to shortcuts that can get a website penalized or removed from the results.

The details

Search engine optimization helps patients find your website and your practice. When patients look for a doctor or a treatment, they turn to Google, map results and, increasingly, AI assistants. Being visible in those places is what brings them to your website.

A well built medical website combines an attractive design with clean, fast code that search engines can read easily. Visibility earned through SEO does not require paying for every click, which can make it more cost effective than advertising over time.

Optimizing a medical website is not a one-time setup. Our staff keeps working on your website on an ongoing basis.

Keywords and search intent

Keywords are the words and questions a patient types or speaks into a search engine or an AI assistant. Our staff builds a list of the terms that matter for your practice and maps each one to the page that answers it best. That work starts with understanding your practice, which we take the time to learn, and a thorough analysis of your competition.

Technical setup

Before we begin designing, we set up your website with the code search engines read: title tags, descriptions, image alt text and structured data for every page. We plan for mobile screens first and for fast loading pages, because both affect how patients and search engines experience your website.

Link building

A link points to your website from another place. Search engines look at the quality and relevance of the websites that link to you, not the number of links. We use ethical, white hat strategies only.

Content

The quality of the information on your website is essential to how it performs in search. Search engines and AI search tools favor content that is accurate, helpful and written for patients. We have physicians on staff who write and review content for your website, your email campaigns and your social media.

Questions we hear

How long does it take for SEO to show results for a medical practice?

It depends on your practice, your specialty, how competitive your local market is and the current state of your website. SEO generally builds over months rather than days, because search engines need time to read and trust the changes. After a free analysis of your site and your competition, we give you a plan with realistic expectations for your situation.

What do you need from us to get started?

We start by learning about your practice: the procedures you offer, the patients you want to reach and the area you serve. From there, our staff builds your keyword list and analyzes your competition, so most of the work falls on us. Your time is mainly needed at the beginning and for reviewing content before it goes live.

Do we have to write the content for our website ourselves?

No. We have physicians on staff who write and review content for your website, as well as for your email campaigns and social media. You can review what we write to make sure it reflects how your practice works.

Is SEO a one-time project or ongoing work?

It is ongoing, and our staff works on your website daily. The heaviest effort comes at the start, when we set up title tags, descriptions, image tags and structured data for every page and build out your content and links. Once the site is optimized correctly, only a small maintenance effort is needed to hold your rankings, which is what makes SEO more cost effective than paying for every click.
