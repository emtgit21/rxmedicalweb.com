Search Engine Optimization is the most effective tool to promote your website and medical practice. At RX Medical Web, we specialize in internet marketing for doctors through the sophisticated use of SEO practices. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the process involved in getting your medical practice website to appear high in Search Engine Rankings. When consumers look for information on the internet, we know that having your medical website appear on top is paramount in generating traffic to your site.

A well-built and well-designed medical website will have an attractive design and look, combined with proper coding for optimization. If designed and programmed correctly, your website will consistently stand out when a customer searches for information on Google™ (or other search engine).

SEO is actually more effective and more cost effective than Pay Per Click because you do not have to pay anything to have your website appear high in the rankings. Once your site has been optimized correctly, only a small maintenance fee is needed to maintain your rankings.

The “Search Engine Optimization” team at Rx Medical Web utilizes the most aggressive, consistent techniques to accomplish first page results for our clients. Optimizing a medical website so that it is found consistently is not a 1-time setup. Our staff optimizes your medical website DAILY with numerous proprietary tools that have proven efficacy.

Search Engine Optimization for Doctors

Our staff at Rx Medical Web is constantly updating their credentials to keep up with Google™’s constantly changing internet Algorithms.

Keywords are the words or phrases that a potential customer types into Google™ (or other search engine) when searching for information. Keywords are programmed into your website and are picked up by Google™ when a consumer types in the phrase. Our staff will comprise a list of keywords for your practice to help ensure your website appears on the first page of the search engines. Part of this development involves a thorough understanding of your medical practice (we spend the time to learn) and a comprehensive analysis of your competition.

Initial Programming of Medical Website
Even before we begin designing your website, we set up and program your website with vital “coding” that will be used by Google™ (and other search engines) to ensure your site appears on the first page of Google™. Each page of your website will contain this essential coding. Some of these codes include programming “Metatags”, “Title Tags”, Image Tags”, and descriptions for each page.
Medical SEO Link Building
A Link is information or content that is directed to your website from another place. Link building is essential in any search engine optimization strategy. The more relevant, incoming links your website contains, the better rankings you will see. We use ethical, “white hat” strategies only.
Effective Content For Medical SEO
“Content is King” has now become a common phrase in the industry. The Quality information on your website is essential in website rankings. We have physicians on staff who write compelling, relevant content for your website as well as for email campaigns, newletters and FaceBook Ads.
