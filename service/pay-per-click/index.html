Pay-per-click marketing

Paid search is the fastest way to the top of the results. Your ad appears when a patient searches for a service you offer.

In plain words

Pay-per-click marketing places a short text ad for your practice above the regular search results when someone nearby searches for a service you offer. You pay only when a person clicks the ad, which is where the name comes from. A practice needs it because it reaches patients at the moment they are looking for care, without waiting months for a website to climb the unpaid results.

This is a good fit if

  • You are opening a new location or adding a service and cannot wait for unpaid search results to build
  • Your schedule has open appointments for specific procedures that you want to fill
  • You want to see which marketing dollars led to actual patient calls and bookings

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Organized ad account

Your account is arranged into separate campaigns for each service, so spending and results for each one stay easy to read.

Budget plan by service

We divide your monthly ad spend among your services according to which ones matter most to your practice right now.

Keyword list

A researched list of the phrases patients type when looking for your services, plus phrases we block to avoid wasted clicks.

Written ads

Several versions of each ad, drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by our team so the wording is accurate and appropriate.

Campaign landing pages

Each campaign gets its own web page that matches the ad and gives visitors one clear way to call or book.

Call and booking reports

Regular reports show how many phone calls and appointment requests the ads produced, not only how many people clicked.

How it works

  1. Discovery call

    We ask about your services, service area, capacity and the monthly budget you are comfortable with. You tell us which procedures you want more patients for and who should approve ad wording.

  2. Account build

    We set up the campaigns, keywords, ads and landing pages, and send them to you for review. You give us access to your website and any existing ad account, and confirm that every claim in the ads is accurate.

  3. Launch and tracking

    We turn the ads on and connect call and booking tracking, set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your compliance contact. Your front desk only needs to answer the phone and handle requests as usual.

  4. Ongoing management

    We review the search terms, adjust bids, test new ad wording and move budget toward what brings in patients. You tell us when your schedule, services or priorities change so the campaigns can follow.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Google Ads

This is the platform where businesses pay to show ads on Google search results. We build and manage your campaigns inside it, and the account stays in your practice's name.

Call tracking numbers

These are phone numbers that forward to your regular line and record which ad led to the call. We place them on your ads and landing pages so every call can be traced to its source.

Website analytics

Analytics software counts what visitors do on your website after they arrive. We use it to see which ads lead to completed booking forms and which lead to visitors leaving.

AI language models

These are software programs that can draft and reword text quickly. We use them to draft ad variations and sort search data, and our team reviews everything before it runs, with physician review for medical content.

Words you will hear

Cost per click
The amount you pay each time someone clicks your ad, which varies with how many other advertisers want the same search phrase.
Quality score
A rating Google gives each keyword based on how well your ad and landing page match the search, which affects your position and cost.
Conversion
A visitor taking the action you want, such as calling the office or submitting an appointment request.

The details

Pay-per-click ads appear above the organic search results. When patients search for terms related to your practice, the ads reach them at that moment and bring them to your website.

Questions we hear

How do you set up and manage a pay-per-click campaign for a medical practice?

We start by organizing the account into a clear structure, assigning budget across campaigns, and building keyword coverage around the services you offer. From there we work on quality score, create a landing page for each campaign, and set up call and booking tracking so we can see which ads bring in patients.

How much does it cost, and how soon will we see patient inquiries?

Both depend on your practice, including your specialty, your local competition, and the services you want to promote. Paid search generally works faster than organic methods because ads can appear as soon as a campaign is live, but we do not promise specific results. We provide a quote and a plan after a free analysis of your practice.

What do you need from our practice to get started?

We need to know which services you want to promote, the area you draw patients from, and how patients currently call or book with you. That lets us structure the account around your priorities and connect call and booking tracking to the way your front desk actually works.

How will we know whether the ads are bringing in patients and not just clicks?

Traffic alone is not the goal, so we measure phone calls and bookings from patients interested in your services. Our results and phone tracking system shows you those calls and bookings directly. It is worth watching these numbers instead of clicks or impressions, since a campaign can generate plenty of visits without producing appointments.
