Physician content marketing

Website content, newsletters, articles and blogs written by physicians and internet writers. We do all the work.

In plain words

Physician content marketing is the written material that represents your practice online: the pages of your website, blog posts, articles, press releases and the newsletters emailed to your patients. Patients and search engines both judge a practice by what it publishes, and AI assistants now quote websites directly, so the writing has to be clear, current and medically accurate. Physicians write or review the medical content, our internet writers prepare it for the web, and your staff is not asked to write anything.

What you get

Swipe through the pieces we deliver.

Custom website pages

Pages about your practice, physicians and services, written from the information you give us so they describe your practice accurately.

Regular new content

Fresh pages and updates added as part of ongoing maintenance, so readers see current information and search engines have something new to index.

Patient email newsletters

Physician-written newsletters emailed to the patients on your list, keeping your practice familiar until they are ready to book a visit.

Blog posts and articles

Educational pieces on the topics your patients ask about, published under your practice name to help people find you through search.

Press releases

Short announcements about news at your practice, such as a new physician, location or service, written and published for you.

Physician-reviewed medical accuracy

Every piece with medical content is written or checked by a physician before it is published, including anything first drafted with AI tools.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Learn your practice

    We talk with you about your services, your patients and what you want to be known for. You provide basic information about the practice, your physicians and any topics you want covered or avoided.

  2. Plan the topics

    We put together a list of pages, articles and newsletter subjects based on what patients search for and what your practice offers. You look over the list and tell us if anything is missing or off the mark.

  3. Write and review

    Our writers research and draft with the help of AI tools, a physician writes or reviews the medical content, and our internet staff makes it search engine friendly. You do not need to write, and you can read pieces before they go out if you wish.

  4. Publish and continue

    We post the content to your website, send the newsletters to your patient list and keep adding new material on a regular schedule. Your part is to tell us about news at the practice, such as a new service or physician.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Content management system

This is the software behind your website that lets pages and blog posts be added or changed without rebuilding the site. We use it to publish and update your content, so your staff never has to log in.

Email marketing platform

This is a service that stores a list of email addresses and sends the same message to everyone on it, with a way to unsubscribe. We use it to send your newsletters to the addresses your website collects, set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your practice's compliance contact.

AI language models

These are computer programs that can research a topic and produce a first draft of text quickly. We use them to assist our writers, and a physician checks medical content before it is published, so nothing goes out on AI output alone.

Search engines and AI assistants

Google and similar search engines, along with AI assistants that answer questions by quoting websites, are how many patients first read about a practice. We write and format your content so these systems can read it easily and represent it accurately.

Words you will hear

Search engine optimization (SEO)
The work of making a website easier for search engines such as Google to find, understand and show to people looking for what you offer.
Indexing
The process by which a search engine reads a web page and adds it to its records so the page can appear in search results.
