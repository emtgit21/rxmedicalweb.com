Physician content marketing

Website content, newsletters, articles and blogs written by physicians and internet writers. We do all the work.

Effective material on your website captures readers' interest and is vital for optimization and internet marketing.

Our writers are physicians and internet staff. A physician writes or reviews the medical content, then our internet staff makes sure it is search engine friendly. Writing for the web is different from writing for journals or print, and with AI assistants now quoting websites directly, accuracy matters more than ever. We use AI tools to research and draft faster, and a physician checks the result.

Website content

Our physicians and writers prepare custom content along with the information you provide. As part of ongoing maintenance, new content is added regularly, both to keep information fresh for readers and to give search engines something new to index.

Email marketing and newsletters

Your website has an information management system that gathers patient email addresses. Our physicians write articles and newsletters that we email to your patients. Patients are always at different stages of a decision, so regular contact means they know who you are, what you offer and how to find you when they are ready.

Articles, press releases and blogs

Publishing articles, press releases and blogs is a vital part of search engine optimization. It is time consuming work that some website firms leave to the physician or the office staff. We do all the work, and we do not ask you to help write.
