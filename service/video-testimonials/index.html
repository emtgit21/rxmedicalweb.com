Medical video testimonials

Our videographers come to your office, interview your patients and deliver a finished video for your website.

In plain words

A medical video testimonial is a short, professionally filmed video in which your real patients describe, in their own words, what it was like to be cared for at your practice. Our videographers come to your office, interview the patients who volunteer and edit the footage into a finished video for your website. New visitors tend to trust a person they can see and hear more than a written quote, which makes them more comfortable contacting you.

What you get

Swipe through the pieces we deliver.

Half-day on-site filming

Our videographers travel to your office and spend half a day filming, so patients and staff stay in familiar surroundings.

Patient preparation and interviews

Our experienced interviewers put your volunteers at ease and ask simple questions, so their answers sound natural instead of rehearsed.

Footage of your office

We also film your office itself, giving future patients a look at the place before they ever walk in.

Professional editing

Our editors select the strongest moments, trim the pauses and combine everything into one clear, well-paced story.

Finished testimonial video

You receive a polished video of real patients describing their experience, ready to place on your website.

Files you own

The video files and final product are yours to keep, and everything is covered by one all-inclusive fee.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Planning conversation

    We talk with you about your practice, the services you want to highlight and a filming date that will not disrupt your schedule. You tell us which rooms we can use and who our day-of contact will be.

  2. Patient invitations

    You invite satisfied patients who are willing to appear on camera and collect their written permission. This step is set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your practice's compliance contact.

  3. Filming day

    Our crew arrives, sets up in the space you have chosen and interviews each volunteer in turn. You only need to keep that space available and have your patients arrive at their scheduled times.

  4. Editing and publishing

    Our editors build the final video and send it to you with the files. Once you confirm it represents your practice accurately, it goes on your website, where it keeps working for you with every new visitor.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Professional cameras and microphones

This is the filming equipment that captures a sharp picture and clear voices, even in a busy office. We bring and operate all of it, so you do not need to buy or rent anything.

Video editing software

These are the programs editors use to cut, arrange and polish raw footage. We use them to correct sound and color, add your practice name and logo, and shape the interviews into one finished piece.

Video hosting platform

This is an online service, such as YouTube or Vimeo, that stores a video and plays it smoothly on any phone or computer. The finished video can be stored there so it loads quickly on your website without slowing your pages down.

Structured data

This is a small piece of behind-the-scenes code that tells search engines like Google that a page contains a video and what it is about. It can be added to the page that holds your testimonial, so the video supports your search engine optimization.

The details

Video testimonials are one of the most effective ways to make a strong first impression on new visitors. They show what sets your practice apart, in your patients' own words.

Watching real patients talk about their experience creates comfort and trust in a way written reviews cannot. A prospective patient who has heard a satisfied patient describe their visit has a clearer reason to contact you.

Words you will hear

B-roll
Extra footage of your office, staff and surroundings that is shown while a patient is speaking to make the video more interesting to watch.
Patient authorization
A signed form in which a patient gives written permission for their image, voice and story to be used in your marketing.
Embedding
Placing a video on a web page so visitors can press play and watch it there without leaving your site.

This is a good fit if

  • You have happy patients who would gladly speak about their experience but nothing on your website that shows them
  • Your website describes your services well but feels impersonal to someone visiting for the first time
  • You offer elective or aesthetic services where new patients want to hear from someone who has already been through it

Questions we hear

How does a filming day at our office work?

Our videographers travel to your office and spend half a day filming patient testimonials along with footage of your practice. Our staff prepares and interviews each patient, so your team does not need to script anything or run the camera. After the shoot, our editors turn the footage into the finished video for your website.

What do we need to provide on our end?

You will need to invite a few satisfied patients who are willing to appear on camera and set aside space in your office for the half day of filming. As with any patient marketing, your practice should obtain each patient's written permission before they are filmed. We take care of the interviewing, filming and editing.

What if our patients are nervous or have never been on camera?

Most patients are not used to being filmed, and that is fine. Our interviewers are experienced and friendly, and they guide each volunteer through a relaxed conversation instead of asking them to memorize lines. That approach is what makes the testimonials come across as natural and believable.

How much does it cost, and who owns the video afterward?

The package is offered for one all-inclusive fee with no additional charges, covering on-site production, patient interviews and post production. Pricing depends on your practice, so we provide a current quote after a free analysis of your needs. The video files and the final product are yours to keep.
