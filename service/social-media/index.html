Social media management

A dedicated team creates and maintains your social media, so your practice stays in front of patients between visits.

In plain words

Social media management means a dedicated team plans, writes, designs and publishes posts on your practice's social networks, and answers the people who respond to them. Patients look for health information and get a feel for a practice on these networks long before they call, so a quiet or outdated profile can cost you their trust. You do not have to use social media yourself for it to work for your practice.

What you get

Swipe through the pieces we deliver.

Written social media strategy

A short plan that names which networks you will use, who you are trying to reach and what topics you will cover.

Competitor and market review

A summary of what similar practices in your area post and where patients in your specialty spend time online.

Branded practice profiles

Your accounts set up or cleaned up with correct contact details, logo, photos and a consistent description of your practice.

Weekly posts

Original photos, short videos and captions published every week, drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by our team and a physician.

Comment and message monitoring

We watch your comments and messages, reply to routine questions and pass anything clinical or sensitive to your staff.

Paid ads and promotions

Paid campaigns for events, new services or special offers, with booking links adjusted so more viewers become appointment requests.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Research

    We talk with you about your goals, services and ideal patients, then study the networks and practices in your market. You tell us which procedures matter most and who should approve content.

  2. Profile setup

    We create or update your accounts and prepare the first batch of posts. You provide account access, your logo, photos of the office and team, and any rules your compliance contact wants followed.

  3. Launch

    We publish the first posts and turn on any agreed ads. You review and approve the opening content so the tone sounds like your practice.

  4. Ongoing weekly posts

    We post every week, respond to your audience and report what is getting attention. You send us news, events and signed patient consent for any photos or stories we use.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Instagram and Facebook

The two networks where most patients browse photos, short videos and local business pages. We publish your posts here and answer the comments and messages they bring in.

Social media scheduling software

A program that lets posts be prepared in advance and published automatically at set times. We use it to keep your accounts active every week without anyone at the practice having to log in.

AI language models

Software that can draft text and suggest ideas from a short instruction. We use it to draft captions and topic lists, then our writers edit them and a physician reviews anything medical before it goes out.

Social media advertising tools

The paid side of the networks, where a post can be shown to people by location, age or interests. We use them to put your events and services in front of nearby people who do not follow you yet.

The details

Whatever your specialty, patients turn to social media for health information and to get a sense of a practice before they book. An effective physician marketing program includes social media, and you do not need to use it personally for it to work for your practice.

Our team of social media professionals focuses entirely on creating and maintaining your channels, including Instagram, TikTok and Facebook where they fit your audience. We develop content for your practice, with AI-assisted drafting and human and physician review, and build a connection with your audience.

Words you will hear

Engagement
The total of likes, comments, shares and saves a post receives, which shows whether people actually paid attention to it.
Reach
The number of different people who saw a post at least once, whether or not they follow you.
Conversion
The moment a viewer takes the action you wanted, such as calling the office or filling out an appointment request form.

This is a good fit if

  • You know your practice should be active on social media but nobody on staff has the time to do it consistently
  • Your profiles exist but have not been updated in months and no longer reflect the practice
  • You offer elective or cash-pay services that patients research and compare online before choosing a provider

Questions we hear

Do I or my staff need to be active on social media for this to work?

No. Our team of social media professionals creates and maintains your networks, so you do not need to use social media personally. We develop the content and handle community management, which keeps your practice in front of patients between visits.

What does the process look like once we start?

We begin by researching the social networks that fit your practice, along with your competitors and local marketplace. From there we set up your profiles, launch, and move into ongoing weekly posts. Strategy, content creation, ad management, and events and promotions are part of each project.

How long does it take to see new patients from social media?

It depends on your practice, your specialty, and where your profiles stand today. Social media generally builds over time, as regular interaction builds confidence and followers gradually become patients. After a free analysis, we provide a plan that outlines what to expect for your situation.

How much does social media management cost?

The cost depends on your practice, including the networks involved and how much ad management and content your plan calls for. We provide a quote after a free analysis of your current presence and goals.
