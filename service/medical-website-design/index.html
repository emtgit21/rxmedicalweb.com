Medical website design

Custom websites for medical, aesthetic and dental practices, designed and programmed in house and built to turn visitors into patient calls.

In plain words

A medical website is your practice's front door online: the place where patients read about your treatments, decide whether they trust you, and call or request an appointment. Almost every patient looks a practice up before booking, and every ad, search result and social post you run sends people back to the site. We design and program each one from scratch so it loads quickly, reads clearly on a phone and makes contacting your office the obvious next step.

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Custom design for your practice

A look created from a blank page around your specialty, your taste and your budget, not a template shared with other practices.

Pages that work on phones

Every page is laid out for a small screen first and loads quickly, because most patients will visit from their phone.

Search engine friendly structure

Pages are organized and labeled so Google can read what you treat and where you are, which helps patients find you.

A site you can edit

You get a simple login where your staff can change text, photos, hours and blog posts without calling a programmer.

Online patient forms

Patients can request appointments or complete paperwork from home, set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your compliance contact.

Call reporting and phone support

You can see the phone calls your website produces, and you can reach our staff by phone when you need help.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Analysis

    We talk through your goals, budget, the treatments you want to grow and the sites you like or dislike. If you already have a website, we review it and tell you what is worth keeping.

  2. Sketch

    Your assigned designer draws the layout of the home page and main pages so you can see the structure before anything is built. You tell us what to change and approve the direction.

  3. Design and development

    Our designers and programmers build the full site and prepare the text, with AI helping on research and first drafts and our team reviewing every page. You provide photos, provider bios and logos, and a physician checks the medical content.

  4. Testing and launch

    We test the site on phones, tablets and computers, check every form and phone number, then publish it once you sign off. After launch we show your staff how to make edits, and you watch the call reports to see what the site brings in.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Content management system

This is the private editing area behind your website, similar to working in a word processor. We set it up and train your staff so routine updates do not depend on us.

Call tracking numbers

These are phone numbers that ring at your front desk as usual but record that the call came from your website. We use them to show you how many patient calls the site produces.

AI language models

These are software tools that can research topics, draft text and help check pages for errors. We use them to speed up the early work, and our team reviews everything before it goes live, with physician review for medical content.

Blog and social media integration

A blog is a section of your site for short articles, and integration means your site links to your social media profiles. We build both in so new articles and posts keep the site current and give patients more ways to reach you.

Words you will hear

Responsive design
A way of building a website so its layout automatically adjusts to fit a phone, tablet or computer screen.
Conversion
The moment a website visitor takes the action you want, such as calling your office or sending an appointment request.
Search engine optimization (SEO)
The work of organizing and writing a website so search engines like Google understand it and show it to people searching for your services.

Questions we hear

How does the website design process work?

We follow four stages: analysis, sketch, design and development, and testing. We start by learning your goals, your budget and your taste, then assign a designer who stays with your project from the first sketch to launch. Our designers and programmers build each site from scratch in house, and people review everything before it goes live.

How much does a medical website cost, and how long does it take to build?

Both depend on your practice, including the number of pages, the features you need and how much content has to be created. We provide a quote and a plan after a free analysis of your goals and your current online presence. Projects usually move faster when your team can review sketches and drafts promptly.

We already have a website. Do we need to start over?

Not necessarily. We can review your current site to see how effective it is and recommend simple changes that suit your practice and your budget. We judge a site by whether patients can find it and whether it brings in revenue, so a full rebuild only makes sense if the existing site falls short on those points.

How will we know if the new website is bringing in patients?

Our results and phone tracking system lets you see the calls your website produces, so you can judge the site on patient inquiries instead of appearance alone. We cannot promise specific rankings or call volumes, since those depend on your market, your competition and the marketing you run alongside the site. What we can do is build with layouts and features that have been shown to generate results and give you a clear view of the calls coming in.
