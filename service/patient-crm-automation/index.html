Lead management and patient CRM

Every inquiry captured, answered in minutes and followed until it becomes a booked patient. Past patients brought back automatically.

Most practices spend to attract an inquiry and then lose it because nobody followed up in time. A CRM keeps every lead and patient in one place and makes the follow-up happen on its own.

Lead management

  • Every form, call, text, chat and social message lands in one place
  • Each lead is tagged with the campaign that produced it
  • Scoring and routing, so your staff calls the hottest leads first
  • We can manage the first response for you, by AI agent or by our team
  • A weekly report: leads in, contacted, booked and lost, with the reasons

We manage leads this way every day for Empire Medical Training, and we bring the same system to your practice.

What we set up

  • One inbox for web forms, calls, texts and chat
  • Instant replies to new inquiries, then a sequence of follow-ups
  • Reminders for patients who are due for a repeat treatment
  • Reactivation campaigns for patients you have not seen in a while
  • Birthday, promotion and event messages
  • A pipeline view: inquiries, consultations booked, treatments done

We connect the CRM to your website, your ads and your call tracking, so you can see which marketing produces patients and not just clicks.
