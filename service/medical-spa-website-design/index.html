Medical spa website design

Websites and marketing for med spas, day spas and aesthetic practices, custom built to match the experience patients get when they walk in.

In plain words

A medical spa website is your practice's online front door: a custom-designed site that presents your treatments, your team and your setting the way patients experience them in person. Most patients look up a practice before they call, so the site has to look right and also be easy to find on search engines and in answers from AI assistants. Our in-house team designs and builds it, then pairs it with the social media and email marketing that bring patients to it.

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Custom designed website

A site designed from scratch around your brand, photos and treatment menu, with no templates, so it looks like your practice.

Physician reviewed treatment pages

Pages explaining each treatment in plain language, drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by our physician staff before they are published.

Online consultation booking

A booking tool on the site that lets patients request consultations and treatments at any hour, without calling your front desk.

Bilingual site version

A second language version of your pages, such as Spanish, so more patients in your area can read about your services.

Social and email campaigns

Social media pages and email newsletters, written with our physician staff, that keep your practice in front of current and prospective patients.

Maintenance and phone support

Ongoing updates, fixes and content changes handled by our staff, with a phone number you can call to reach a person.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Goals conversation

    We ask about your practice, the treatments you want to grow and the patients you want to reach. You tell us your priorities and share any existing logo, colors, photos and website access.

  2. Design and content

    We present a design for the site and draft the text for each page. You review both, correct anything that does not sound like your practice, and approve the treatment descriptions.

  3. Build and connect

    Our programmers build the approved design and connect booking, forms and your electronic medical record system where needed. Anything that touches patient data is set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your compliance contact, so we need that person available.

  4. Launch and upkeep

    We publish the site, then keep it maintained and adjust it as search engines and AI assistants change how they show results. You send us news, new treatments, staff changes and photos as they come up.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Content management system

The software behind a website that stores its pages, text and images and lets them be edited without rebuilding the site. We build your site on one so updates such as a new treatment or a new provider can be made quickly.

Online booking system

A scheduling tool that shows patients available times and takes their appointment requests through the website. We add it to your site and link it to the calendar or practice software your staff already uses, where that is possible.

Structured data

Labels added to a page's code, invisible to visitors, that tell search engines exactly what the page contains, such as your address, hours and services. We add them so Google and AI assistants can describe your practice accurately.

AI language models

Software that can draft and summarize text, and that now answers many patient questions directly in search results. We use it to assist with first drafts and research, and our writers and physician staff review everything before it goes live. It never gives medical advice to patients.

Questions we hear

How long does it take to build a med spa website, and what does it cost?

Both depend on the practice: the number of treatments you offer, whether you need a bilingual site, and whether you want online booking, EMR integration or ongoing marketing alongside the site. We start with a free analysis of your practice and goals, then give you a quote and a plan that lays out the scope and schedule.

What do we need to provide to get the project started?

Mainly your goals, a clear list of the treatments and services you offer, and a sense of the experience patients have when they walk into your practice, since the site is custom built to match it. You do not have to write everything yourself, because our physician staff helps write the content. Photos of your space, team and branding are useful if you have them, as they help the design reflect your actual practice.

We are a family practice just adding aesthetic services. Is this a fit for us, or only for established med spas?

It fits both. We work with plastic surgeons, dermatologists and family practitioners who are adding aesthetics, as well as established skin care and aesthetics centers. The strategy is designed after we learn your goals, so a practice introducing a few treatments gets a different plan than a full service med spa.

What should we watch out for when comparing agencies for a med spa website?

Ask whether the site is built from a template, whether the work is outsourced, and who writes the content and supports the site after launch. We do not use templates or outsource, our designers, programmers and internet specialists work in house, and maintenance and phone support with our staff are included. Be cautious of anyone who promises specific search rankings, since search engines change their algorithms constantly and AI assistants now answer many patient questions directly, so no agency controls those results.
