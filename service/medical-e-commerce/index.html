Online medical stores and shopping carts

Sell skincare, supplements and other products from your practice website. We set up the store, the payments and the products.

In plain words

An online medical store is a section of your practice website where patients can browse the skincare, supplements and other products you recommend, pay by card and have them shipped or set aside for pickup. It lets patients reorder between visits without calling the front desk, and it keeps those purchases with your practice instead of a general retailer. Our programmers build the store, connect the payments and load your products, so your staff does not have to learn e-commerce to get started.

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Store built into your website

A shop section designed with the same colors, fonts and layout as your practice website, so patients know they are still with you.

Custom shopping cart

The part of the store that holds what a patient has chosen, totals the order and walks them through paying for it.

Merchant account and payments

We set up the account that lets your practice accept cards and digital wallets online, with the money deposited to your bank.

Product pages

One page for each product with photos, a clear description, size options and price, written from the information you give us and approved by you.

Subscriptions and repeat orders

An option for patients to receive a product they use regularly on a schedule, charged automatically until they pause or cancel it.

Hosting and maintenance

We keep the store online, apply software updates, fix problems and make product or price changes when you send them to us.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Plan the store

    We talk through what you want to sell, how you want to be paid and how orders will reach patients. You provide your product list, prices and any rules your suppliers set for selling online.

  2. Build and connect

    We design the store, build the cart and open or connect your merchant account. You supply business and bank details for the payment application and decide on shipping, pickup and return policies.

  3. Load and test

    We add your products, then place test orders to confirm that payments, taxes, shipping charges and confirmation emails all work. You review every product page and the checkout before anything goes public.

  4. Launch and maintain

    We open the store to patients and show your staff how to see and fill orders. After that we host and maintain it, and you tell us when products, prices or stock change.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

E-commerce platform

This is the software that runs the store: it displays products, manages the cart and records each order. We install it on your practice website and configure it so your staff can look up orders in one place.

Payment gateway

A payment gateway is the service that takes the card details a patient types in and passes them safely to the bank for approval. We connect it to your checkout so card numbers are handled by the payment processor and are not stored on your website.

Digital wallets

Digital wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay let a patient pay with a card already saved on their phone or computer. We add them at checkout so patients on a phone can pay without typing a card number.

SSL certificate

An SSL certificate encrypts the information that travels between a patient's browser and your website, shown by the padlock in the address bar. We install it and keep it current across the store and checkout.

Words you will hear

Merchant account
A type of bank account that allows a business to accept card payments and receive the money from those sales.
Checkout
The final pages of an online store where the buyer enters a shipping address and payment details and confirms the order.
PCI compliance
The card industry's security rules for any business that accepts card payments, which apply to how the payment information is collected and handled.

This is a good fit if

  • You already recommend or sell products in the office and patients ask how to get more between visits
  • Your front desk spends time taking reorders by phone and you would like those orders to come in online
  • You want to sell products under your practice's name but do not have staff who can build or run an online store

Questions we hear

What do we need to provide to get the store started?

We need your product list with photos, descriptions and prices, plus a decision on which payment methods you want to accept. If you do not have product photos or written descriptions yet, tell us and we will plan for that as part of the setup. We handle the store design, the merchant account and loading the products.

Does the store live on our current practice website or on a separate site?

We build the shopping cart into your practice website, and we design the store to match the look of the rest of your site. Patients can move from reading about your services to buying a product without feeling like they left your practice. This also lets you promote products on the same pages patients already visit.

How long does it take to launch, and what does it cost?

Both depend on your practice: the number of products, the payment options you want, whether you need subscriptions, and the condition of your current website. We start with a free analysis and then give you a quote and a plan with the steps involved.

What should we watch out for before selling products online?

Think through the day-to-day work first: who will pack and ship orders, how you will handle returns, and who keeps inventory and prices current. Sales tax and rules on shipping certain products vary by state, so it is worth checking with your accountant or attorney before launch. On our side, we host and maintain the store, and for items patients buy regularly we can set up subscriptions and repeat orders.
