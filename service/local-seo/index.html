Local search engine optimization

Most patients choose a practice near them. Local SEO puts you on the map, with listings that are complete and correct.

In plain words

Local search engine optimization (local SEO) is the work of making your practice appear when someone nearby searches for a doctor, a dentist or a treatment you offer, whether on Google, on Google Maps or in medical directories. Search engines compare your name, address, phone number and hours across many websites, and listings that are missing, duplicated or wrong make them less confident about showing you. We correct those listings first, then build out your profiles and pages so patients in your area can find you and reach you.

This is a good fit if

  • You have moved, changed phone numbers or added a location, and old details still appear online
  • Your patients come mostly from the surrounding area and other practices appear above you on the map
  • You have never claimed your Google profile or are not sure who controls it

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Listing audit and cleanup

A report of every listing we find for your practice, with duplicates removed and wrong details corrected.

Directory submissions

Your correct practice details submitted to the major data directories that supply information to maps, apps and voice assistants.

Google Business Profile

A complete, verified profile with your services, hours, photos and categories, which we keep current as things change.

Medical directory listings

Profiles on the health directories where patients look up providers, claimed and filled in with accurate information for each practitioner.

Location and service pages

A page on your website for each office and main service, written so nearby searches lead to the right page.

One-tap contact buttons

Buttons on your pages and profiles that let a patient on a phone call you or request an appointment in one tap.

How it works

  1. Intake conversation

    We ask about your locations, providers, services and the areas you want to draw patients from. You give us your current addresses, phone numbers and hours, plus access to any Google profile you already have.

  2. Audit and cleanup

    We search for every existing listing of your practice and fix or remove the ones that are incomplete, duplicated or wrong. You may need to answer a verification call, email or postcard from Google or a directory.

  3. Build and publish

    We complete your profiles, submit your details, write the location and service pages and add the contact buttons. You approve the pages before they go live, and anything that connects to online booking or a form is set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your compliance contact.

  4. Ongoing upkeep

    We watch for new duplicates and unwanted edits, keep your listings current and share what we see in calls, direction requests and local searches. You tell us when hours, providers or services change.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Google Business Profile

The free listing Google gives every business, which appears as the information panel patients see in search results. We use it as the main record of your practice and update it whenever something changes.

Google Maps

The map app and map results many patients use to find a nearby practice and get directions. We make sure each location has its own entry and that the pin sits at the right address and entrance.

Structured data

Small labels added to your website's code that tell search engines plainly what your practice is, where it is and when it is open. We add them to your pages so the details match your listings exactly.

AI language models

Software that can compare and draft text quickly. We use it to help spot inconsistent listings and draft page copy, then our team reviews everything and a physician reviews medical content. It never gives medical advice.

Words you will hear

NAP consistency
NAP stands for name, address and phone number, and consistency means those three details are written the same way everywhere your practice is listed.
Citation
Any mention of your practice's name, address and phone number on another website, such as a directory or a review site.
Local pack
The small group of businesses that Google shows next to a map at the top of the results for a nearby search.

Questions we hear

How is your approach to local SEO different from simply submitting our practice to directories?

Many companies only send your information to the major data directories and stop there. We first look for the incomplete, duplicate or wrong listings of your practice that already exist, because those can hurt your rankings. Once they are fixed, we build your directory listings, Google Business Profile and location pages on a clean base.

How long does it take before we see a difference in local search?

It depends on your practice, your area and the condition of your current listings. Corrections to listings can take time to be picked up across directories and by search engines, so local SEO generally builds gradually and does not change overnight. After a free analysis, we give you a plan that reflects your starting point.

What do you need from our practice to get started?

We need your accurate practice details, such as the exact name, address, phone number, hours and services for each location. Access to your existing Google Business Profile, if you have one, lets us update it without creating a duplicate. It also helps to tell us about past addresses, old phone numbers or former practice names, since these are a common source of wrong listings.

We have more than one location and offer several services. Can local SEO handle that?

Yes. We build landing pages for each location and each service, so a patient searching in a specific area finds the page that matches what they are looking for. Each location also needs its own complete and correct listings, and we add call-to-action buttons that let patients call or book in one tap.
