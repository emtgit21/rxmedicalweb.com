The medical practice marketing kit

Our most complete program: a custom website, email capture, print materials, staff training and consultation with physicians and business staff.

In plain words

The medical practice marketing kit is a complete marketing program in one package: a custom website built so patients can find you in search results, the printed materials for your office, a training manual for your front desk, and the consent and treatment forms for your procedures. A practice needs all of these to work together, because a website that brings in calls does little good if the phone is answered poorly or the paperwork is incomplete. You also get ongoing access to our physicians and business staff for advice as the practice grows.

What you get ℞

Custom practice website

A website designed and coded for your practice, not a template, that works on phones and is built so search engines can read it.

Email capture and e-books

Sign-up forms and physician-written e-books that visitors download in exchange for their email address, building a contact list you own.

Online patient forms

Forms patients complete on your website before a visit, set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your compliance contact.

Front Office Fundamentals manual

Our employee manual that teaches your front desk staff how to handle new patient calls, answer questions and book consultations.

Consent and treatment documents

Consent forms, physician procedure notes, diagrams and pre and post treatment instructions for the procedures you perform, ready for your own review.

Office print portfolio

A practice brochure, patient information and referral brochures, a 6×9 services postcard and business cards, all designed to match each other.

This is a good fit if

  • You are opening a new practice or location and need the website, print materials and forms in place together
  • Your current website looks fine but brings in few new patient inquiries, and your printed materials do not match it
  • You are adding aesthetic or elective procedures and your front desk has not been trained to handle calls from patients comparing providers

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Learn your practice

    We talk with you about your specialty, the procedures you want to grow and the patients you want to reach. You provide your background, service list, logo, photos and any existing materials.

  2. Design and write

    Our designers, programmers, writers and videographers build the website and print pieces, with AI assisting on drafts and our staff and physicians reviewing the medical content. You review the drafts and tell us what to correct.

  3. Launch and train

    We publish the website, deliver the print files and forms, and hand your team the front office manual. You set aside time for your staff to study it and name one person to manage website updates.

  4. Ongoing consultation

    We stay available to review how the website and inquiries are performing and to advise on next steps. You bring us your questions, new procedures and any changes in the practice so the materials stay current.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Content management system

This is the software behind your website that lets someone edit pages without knowing how to code. We set it up so your staff can change hours, add a provider or publish a blog post on their own.

Search engine optimization

This is the practice of structuring and writing a website so Google and other search engines understand what you offer and where. We build it into the code, page titles and content from the start instead of adding it afterward.

Email database

This is an organized list of the people who gave you their email address through your website. We connect it to your sign-up forms and e-book downloads so you can send news and promotions to people who asked to hear from you.

Website video

These are short professionally filmed clips of you, your office and your services placed on key pages. Our staff videographers produce them because visitors who see and hear the physician are more comfortable making a first call.

Questions we hear

What is included in the Medical Practice Marketing Kit?

The kit is designed to work as your entire marketing program. It includes a custom website with email capture, an integrated blog and online patient forms, print materials such as brochures, a services postcard and business cards, our Front Office Fundamentals staff manual, consent and treatment forms, and ongoing consultation with our physicians and business staff.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Both depend on your practice, including your specialty, the number of procedures you offer and how much material you already have. We start with a free analysis and then give you a quote and a plan that fit your situation.

What will we need to provide to get started?

We will need accurate information about your background, your services and the procedures you perform, since the website, brochures and consent forms are all built around them. It also helps to have someone in the practice who can review drafts and answer questions, and to set aside time for your front office staff to work through the training manual.

Our practice already has a website. Why would we need a new one?

Many websites look attractive but are poorly coded, which makes them hard for patients to find in search. The websites we build are custom rather than templates, and they are developed by designers, programmers and SEO staff with search visibility in mind from the start. We cannot promise specific rankings, but a well-built site gives your practice a stronger foundation and room to expand as technology changes.
