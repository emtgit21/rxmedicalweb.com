Search engine marketing and social advertising

In crowded markets, SEO alone may not be enough. Paid advertising on Google, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok fills the gap.

In plain words

Paid advertising places your practice at the top of Google search results and in the feeds of people on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, and you pay the platform each time someone clicks or views your ad. Unpaid search rankings (SEO) build slowly and may not be enough in a crowded market, so paid ads put you in front of patients as soon as a campaign starts. We can set up the account and teach your team to run it, or manage it for you.

This is a good fit if

  • Your market is crowded and competing practices already appear above you in search results
  • You are opening a location or adding a service and cannot wait for unpaid rankings to build
  • You have a set monthly ad budget and front desk staff ready to answer new inquiries promptly

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Advertising accounts you own

Accounts on Google and the social platforms, set up in your practice's name so the account and its history stay yours.

Keyword and audience plan

A written list of the search phrases we will bid on and the groups of people each social campaign will reach.

Ads and creative

The written ads, images and short videos for each platform, drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by our team before anything runs.

Retargeting campaigns

Follow-up ads shown to people who visited your website but did not book, keeping your practice in view while they decide.

Seasonal and event campaigns

Short campaigns built around promotions, new services or events, planned ahead so they start and stop on the dates you choose.

Training or ongoing management

Either working sessions that teach your staff to create ads and maintain the account, or regular management and plain-language reports from us.

How it works

  1. First conversation

    We ask about your services, your area, your competitors and what you want the ads to achieve. You tell us which treatments matter most and what monthly ad budget you are comfortable with.

  2. Account setup

    We build the accounts, structure the campaigns and connect them to your website so results can be measured. You give us website access, and anything that touches patient data is set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your compliance contact.

  3. Creative and launch

    We produce the ads and check them against each platform's rules for health and aesthetic advertising. You review and approve the wording, images and offers before anything goes live.

  4. Ongoing adjustment

    We watch where the budget goes and move it toward the ads that bring inquiries, much as a broker manages a portfolio. You tell us which inquiries became patients, or your trained staff takes over the account with us available for questions.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Google Ads

Google's system for buying the sponsored listings that appear above the regular search results. We use it to show your practice when someone nearby searches a phrase such as "Botox Fort Lauderdale".

Instagram and Facebook ads

Paid posts and videos that appear in people's feeds and stories, managed from one shared advertising account. We use them to reach people by location and interests, and they often cost less per click than search.

TikTok ads

Short paid videos that appear between the regular videos people scroll through on TikTok. We use them mainly for aesthetic and wellness services, where a brief, visual introduction to the practice works well.

AI creative testing

Software that helps draft many versions of an ad, with different headlines, images and wording, and compares how each performs. Our team reviews every version before it runs and keeps the ones that bring inquiries.

Words you will hear

Bid
The most you agree to pay for one click on your ad, which the platform weighs against other advertisers to decide whose ad is shown.
Lookalike audience
A group of new people the platform finds because they resemble your best existing patients, built without sharing health information.
Conversion
The action you want a person to take after clicking an ad, such as calling the office or submitting a booking request.

The details

Sometimes a sound SEO plan needs a paid advertising strategy beside it. We can set it up and show you how to create ads, bid on keywords and maintain your own account, or we can manage it for you.

The benefits

  • High placement means more visibility and more traffic
  • Cost effective
  • Highly targeted traffic
  • Ongoing marketing to potential patients
  • High return on investment

Google Ads

With paid search, your website appears at the top of the results page the moment the campaign starts. When a patient types a phrase such as “Botox Fort Lauderdale”, your ad is visible immediately. Each click uses part of your budget, which is paid to Google, so the setup matters. Think of a broker managing a portfolio: campaign management works the same way.

Social advertising

Paid campaigns on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok are also very effective and often cost less per click than search. We build the audiences, produce the creative, and follow each platform's rules for health and aesthetic advertising.

Advanced campaigns

  • Retargeting for visitors who did not book
  • Lookalike audiences built from your best patients, without sharing health information
  • Seasonal promotions and event campaigns
  • AI-assisted creative testing across many versions of an ad
