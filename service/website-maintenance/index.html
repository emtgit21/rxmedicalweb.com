Website security and maintenance

A designated team keeps your website current, secure and fast, and makes your changes within 24 to 48 hours.

In plain words

Website security and maintenance is the ongoing care that keeps your practice website online, protected, accurate and quick to load after it launches. A website runs on software that needs regular updates, and practice details such as hours, providers and services change often. A designated team handles all of it for you and makes the changes you request within 24 to 48 hours.

What you get

The concrete pieces we deliver, in plain language.

Managed website hosting

We keep your website on a server we monitor, so it stays online and loads quickly for patients, including any online store.

Security updates and backups

We install software updates that close known security gaps and keep saved copies of your site so it can be restored.

Changes within 24 to 48 hours

Send us new hours, a new provider, updated photos or a new service page, and we make the change for you.

Broken link and page checks

We look for links, images and pages that have stopped working and fix them before patients or search engines run into them.

Onsite search engine optimization

We keep refining page titles, descriptions and content so search engines such as Google understand what your practice offers and where.

Reports and direct support

You receive marketing reports showing how people find and use your site, and our team answers questions and explains the numbers.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Review your site

    We talk through how your website is built, where it is hosted and what has been causing trouble. You tell us who at the practice will send requests and approve changes.

  2. Set up access

    We move the site to our hosting or connect to your current setup, then put backups, security updates and monitoring in place. You provide logins for the website, the domain name and any online store.

  3. Handle your requests

    You send changes by email or phone, and we complete them within 24 to 48 hours. For anything medical, your team confirms the wording is accurate before it goes live.

  4. Maintain and report

    We check the site daily, apply updates, fix what breaks and keep improving how it appears in search. You read the reports and tell us what is changing at the practice.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Content management system

This is the software your website is built on, such as WordPress, which lets pages be edited without rebuilding the site. We keep it and its add-ons updated and use it to make your changes.

Web hosting and SSL certificate

Hosting is the computer that stores your website and delivers it to visitors, and the SSL certificate is what puts the padlock in the browser's address bar. We manage both, and any form that collects patient information is set up with HIPAA requirements in mind and with your practice's compliance contact.

Automated backups

A backup system saves copies of your whole website on a schedule and stores them apart from the live site. If an update goes wrong or the site is attacked, we use a recent copy to bring it back.

Google Analytics and Google Search Console

These are free Google tools that show how many people visit your site, which pages they read and which searches bring them there. We use them to build your reports and to spot errors Google finds on your pages.

The details

A website that markets your practice well needs accurate information, regular updates and ongoing attention to speed, security and search visibility, both in Google and in AI search.

Our team understands how important this is and assigns people to maintain your website daily.

Words you will hear

Plugin
A small add-on program that gives a website an extra feature, such as an appointment form, and that needs regular updates to stay secure.
Domain name
The address people type to reach your website, such as yourpractice.com, which is registered and renewed separately from the website itself.
Malware
Harmful software that attackers place on a website to steal information, send visitors elsewhere or damage the site.

This is a good fit if

  • Your website still shows old hours, former providers or services you no longer offer because no one has time to update it
  • You do not know who would fix your site if it went down, was hacked or lost its content
  • You want one team to call for hosting, updates, changes and questions instead of several separate vendors

Questions we hear

What do we need to send you when we want something changed on our website?

Tell our support team what you want updated and send any details only your practice has, such as new provider information, service descriptions, office hours or photos. We handle the website modifications, image updates and content additions from there. The clearer the request, the less back and forth is needed before the change goes live.

What does the security side of the service cover?

We apply security updates to your website and keep backups of it, so there is a recent copy to restore from if something goes wrong. We also check regularly for broken links, images and pages. No website can be made completely immune to attacks, but outdated software is one of the most common ways sites get compromised, and keeping it current reduces that risk.

Our website is already built. Why does it need ongoing maintenance?

Search engines tend to favor sites that are accurate, up to date and working properly, and a site left alone slowly collects outdated information, broken links and unpatched software. Our maintenance includes onsite search engine optimization, content additions and regular checks to keep the site in good shape. We also provide marketing reports and analytics so you can see how the site is performing.

How much does website maintenance cost?

It depends on your practice, including the size of your website and whether you sell products online and need e-commerce hosting and maintenance. We start with a free analysis of your current site and then give you a quote based on what it needs.
