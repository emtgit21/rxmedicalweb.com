E-mail marketing management

Stay in contact with your patients with newsletters written by physicians and sent on the schedule you choose.

Email campaigns are a vital part of your internet marketing plan. We build an information management system that stores your patients' names and email addresses, because staying in contact with patients is part of any effective campaign.

Managed for you

  • List setup, clean-up and segmentation by treatment and interest
  • Templates designed to match your brand
  • A monthly calendar of newsletters, promotions and event invitations
  • Automations: welcome series, post-visit follow-up, birthdays and win-back
  • Deliverability set up correctly, so your emails reach the inbox
  • Reports on opens, clicks and the bookings each campaign produced

This is the same email operation we run for Empire Medical Training, sized for a practice.

Physician written emails

Our physicians write and review fresh, informative newsletters that are emailed to your list. You set the interval and can review or change the content. These emails are not sales pitches. They are information your patients will enjoy receiving, and that ongoing contact helps them remember who you are, what services you offer and how to reach you when they are ready for a procedure.

Email capture system

Our email capture system saves a visitor's email address when they request information from you, such as a free report on healthy skin. The reports are written by our physicians and built into your website. Once the visitor enters an email address, the report is sent, and you can keep in touch with newsletters they will be happy to receive.
