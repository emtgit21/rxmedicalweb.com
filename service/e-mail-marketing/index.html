E-mail marketing management

Stay in contact with your patients with newsletters written by physicians and sent on the schedule you choose.

In plain words

Email marketing management is a regular, planned flow of emails from your practice to your patients: newsletters, occasional promotions, event invitations and automatic messages such as a welcome note or a birthday greeting. We run the whole operation for you, from the patient list to the writing, sending and reporting. Patients who hear from you with useful information remember who you are, what you offer and how to reach you when they are ready to book.

What you get

Swipe through the pieces we deliver.

A clean, organized patient list

We set up your contact list, remove bad or duplicate addresses and group patients by treatment and interest so messages stay relevant.

Branded email templates

We design reusable email layouts with your logo, colors and contact details, so every message looks like it came from your practice.

Physician-written newsletters

Our physicians write and review informative newsletters for your patients, and you can read, change or approve each one before it goes out.

A monthly sending calendar

You receive a monthly plan showing which newsletters, promotions and event invitations go out and when, at the interval you choose.

Automatic follow-up emails

We build emails that send themselves at the right moment: a welcome series, post-visit follow-up, birthday greetings and notes to patients who have not returned.

Email capture on your website

Visitors enter their email address to receive a free physician-written report, such as one on healthy skin, and join your list.

How it works

From the first conversation to ongoing results.

  1. Discovery and access

    We talk through your services, your patients and how often you want to send. You provide your current patient contact list, your brand materials and the name of your compliance contact, so that patient data is handled with HIPAA requirements in mind.

  2. Setup

    We prepare the list, design the templates, build the automatic emails and configure your sending domain so messages are recognized as coming from you. You approve the designs and confirm which patient groups make sense for your practice.

  3. Writing and review

    AI helps us draft and organize, our physicians write and review the medical content, and our team prepares each email. You review the calendar and can edit or decline anything before it is sent.

  4. Sending and reporting

    We send on the agreed schedule and report on who opened, who clicked and which bookings each campaign produced. We use those results to adjust topics and timing, and you tell us about new services or events to include.

The tools involved, explained

What each one is, and how we use it for your practice.

Email marketing platform

This is the software that stores your contact list, sends email to many people at once and records what happens to each message. We set it up and manage it for you, so your staff does not need to learn it.

Email authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC)

These are settings on your web domain that prove to Gmail, Outlook and other providers that an email really comes from your practice. We configure them so your messages are more likely to reach the inbox and not the spam folder.

Website sign-up forms

These are short forms on your website where a visitor types an email address to request a free report. We build them into your site and connect them to your list, so each new contact is saved and receives the report automatically.

AI language models

These are software tools that can draft and summarize text. We use them to assist with drafts and subject line ideas, and every email is reviewed by our team and by physicians before it is sent; AI never gives medical advice to your patients.

The details

Email remains one of the most direct ways to stay in contact with your patients, and it is a channel your practice owns. We keep your patients' names and email addresses in a patient CRM, organized so that each message goes to the people it is relevant to.

Words you will hear

Segmentation
Dividing your contact list into groups, for example by treatment received or stated interest, so each group gets emails that apply to them.
Deliverability
A measure of whether your emails actually arrive in the inbox, as opposed to being blocked or sent to spam.
Open and click rates
The share of recipients who opened an email and the share who clicked a link inside it, which together show how much interest a message produced.

This is a good fit if

  • You have years of patient email addresses in your records and rarely or never write to them
  • Your patients come in for one treatment and often do not know about the other services you offer
  • You want to stay in regular contact with patients but nobody on your staff has time to write and send emails

Questions we hear

Who writes the newsletters, and do we get a say in what goes out?

Our physicians write and review each newsletter, so the content is informative rather than a sales pitch. You set how often emails are sent, and you can review or change the content before it goes to your list.

What do we need to provide to get started?

We mainly need your current patient email list, your brand materials such as logo and colors, and an idea of the treatments and promotions you want to feature. From there we handle list setup, clean-up and segmentation by treatment and interest, design templates that match your brand, and set up deliverability so your emails reach the inbox.

How much does email marketing management cost, and how long does setup take?

Both depend on your practice, including the size and condition of your list, how often you want to send, and which automations you need. We start with a free analysis and then give you a quote and a plan that fits your situation.

How will we know whether the emails are bringing in appointments?

We send reports that show opens, clicks and the bookings each campaign produced, so you can see which newsletters, promotions and event invitations patients respond to. Keep in mind that email works through steady contact over time, so results are best judged across several campaigns instead of a single send.
