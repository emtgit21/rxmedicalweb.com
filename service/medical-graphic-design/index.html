Medical branding and graphic design

Logos, brochures, postcards and print materials that give your practice one consistent, professional image.

Our graphic design team has the creativity and the experience to deliver graphics that will not exceed your budget, only your expectations. Professionally designed brochures and print materials get your patients' attention and present a consistent image.

Logo and brand identity

Our designers create an original logo consistent with your practice's theme and overall look, along with the colors, type and templates that keep everything you publish looking like you.

Medical practice brochures

Patients expect information about your credentials, your practice and your services, and it is an essential marketing tool. Our staff writes the information about your background and services, then our design team creates a custom brochure with your logo and practice details.

Patient information brochures

Brochures that explain an individual service your patient may be interested in. Our physician staff can write this material for you.

Patient referral brochures

Referrals are an important part of promoting your practice. We create customized referral brochures for your staff to give to patients, who pass them to friends and family. They detail your credentials, your services and any special promotions.

Services postcards

An attractive 6×9 postcard listing your services, practice information, address and phone number, along with any special promotions. Larger than a business card and easy to keep as a quick reference.
