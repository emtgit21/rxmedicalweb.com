What makes this specialty different to market.

We start from how your patients decide, not from a list of services.

  1. Long research before the first call

    Patients read about symptoms, labs and options for weeks before they contact anyone, and they compare several practices along the way. If your site does not answer their questions in plain language, they finish their research somewhere else.

  2. Strict limits on claims and ads

    Advertising platforms restrict how prescription treatments and health conditions can be promoted, and regulators watch claims about hormones and peptides closely. Ads and pages written without that in mind get disapproved or put the practice at risk.

  3. Cash-pay revenue depends on retention

    Without insurance bringing patients back, the practice relies on memberships, follow-up labs and refills staying on schedule. A new patient who drifts away after the first visit costs far more to replace than to keep.

Advertising with care

  • Claims about what hormone therapy, peptides or IV therapy can do must be accurate and supportable, and words such as safe, proven or anti-aging results invite scrutiny, so we write with these limits in mind and the practice confirms final language with its counsel.
  • Advertising prescription drugs and compounded medications is subject to federal and state rules as well as each ad platform's own policies, which may restrict drug names and require certifications, so we build campaigns with these in mind and the practice should confirm the requirements with its counsel or compliance contact.
  • Testimonials, before-and-after photos and replies to reviews need written patient consent and must never confirm that someone is a patient or reveal health details, so we handle them with HIPAA requirements in mind and the practice confirms its policies with counsel.

Questions we hear from hormone therapy and anti-aging

Can you advertise testosterone and other prescription treatments on Google and Meta?

Each platform has its own policies on prescription drug terms, and some require the advertiser to hold specific certifications. We structure campaigns around consultations, symptoms education and services in ways that fit those policies, and we tell you plainly what is and is not allowed. The practice should confirm regulatory questions with its counsel.

Who writes our medical content?

AI assists with research and first drafts, and our in-house writers shape each piece. Physicians review medical content before it is published, and you approve everything that carries your name.

Can you help us sell and retain wellness memberships?

Yes. We present memberships clearly on your site, build pages and follow-up for people who are considering one, and run e-mail and text campaigns that keep current members informed and engaged. We can also connect online payments and booking to your existing systems.

We are adding peptides or IV therapy after a training course. Can you help us launch it?

Yes. As the strategic marketing partner of Empire Medical Training, we work often with physicians adding a new service after a course. We prepare the treatment page, announcements to your current patients and an initial campaign, with claims reviewed carefully.
