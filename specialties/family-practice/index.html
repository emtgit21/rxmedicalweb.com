What makes this specialty different to market.

We start from how your patients decide, not from a list of services.

  1. Two audiences, one practice

    Insured patients looking for a family doctor and cash patients looking for weight loss or aesthetics search differently and decide differently. Your marketing has to speak to each without confusing the other or diluting the trust you have built as the family doctor.

  2. Existing patients do not know

    Most of your patients have no idea you now offer a new service, and many are already buying it somewhere else. The fastest growth for a new service line often comes from your own panel, which means it depends on e-mail, text and what your staff says in the room.

  3. A front desk already at capacity

    Your phones are busy with refills, referrals and scheduling, so new-patient calls and cash-service inquiries wait or go unanswered. Marketing that produces more calls without a way to answer them only adds pressure on your staff.

Advertising with care

  • Advertising for weight loss and hormone services often involves prescription drugs, and the rules on naming medications, describing compounded products and stating expected results are specific, so we write campaigns with these in mind and your practice should confirm the details with its counsel.
  • Before-and-after photos and patient testimonials for aesthetic or weight loss services call for written consent and careful wording about individual results, and we build campaigns with these points in mind while you confirm requirements with your counsel or compliance contact.
  • Marketing to your existing patients by e-mail or text, and replying to online reviews, both touch patient privacy, so we handle lists and replies with HIPAA requirements in mind and recommend your practice confirm its policies with counsel.

Questions we hear from family practice

We are adding weight loss or aesthetics. Should it have its own website and brand?

For most primary care practices we recommend keeping the new service on your main site with its own section, because your reputation as the family doctor is the reason patients will trust you with it. A separate brand can make sense if you plan a distinct location or a full med spa. We talk through both options with you before anything is built.

Our schedule is nearly full. Is marketing still worth it?

Yes, but the goal changes. Instead of more volume across the board, we focus on the patients and services you have room for, such as a new provider's panel, a specific payer mix or cash services that fit into set blocks of time.

Can your AI agents answer our phones and messages?

Our AI agents can answer by chat, text and phone around the clock to handle questions about hours, insurance accepted, services and booking, then hand off to your staff when a person is needed. They never give medical advice, and conversations are handled with HIPAA requirements in mind.

Who writes the medical content for our site?

AI assists with drafts, and our in-house writers and physicians review and edit everything before it reaches you. You approve the final copy, so it reflects how you practice and what you actually offer.
