What makes this specialty different to market.

We start from how your patients decide, not from a list of services.

  1. Competing with national telehealth brands

    Large online companies advertise heavily for the same searches your patients use. A local practice has to show clearly what it offers that a subscription website does not, such as in-person visits, lab work and a physician who knows the patient.

  2. Advertising rules for prescription medications

    Ad platforms and regulators treat prescription drug names, compounded medications and weight loss claims with extra scrutiny. Campaigns written without these rules in mind get disapproved, or put the practice at risk.

  3. Keeping patients in the program

    Most weight loss programs run on monthly visits or memberships, so the value of a patient depends on how long they stay. Marketing that stops at the first appointment leaves the practice refilling the same seats every month.

Advertising with care

  • Advertising that names prescription medications, including GLP-1 drugs and compounded versions, is subject to platform policies and to federal and state rules on drug promotion, so we build campaigns with these in mind and your practice should confirm specifics with its counsel.
  • Claims about how much weight patients lose, before-and-after photos and testimonials need written patient consent, typical-result context and care not to imply a guaranteed outcome, and we prepare this material with those points in mind while your practice confirms the final wording with counsel.
  • Replies to online reviews must never confirm that someone is a patient or mention their treatment, and membership or subscription billing terms should be disclosed clearly in your marketing, both of which we account for in our work and your practice should verify with its compliance contact.

Questions we hear from medical weight loss

Can you advertise GLP-1 medications on Google and Meta for us?

Each platform has its own policies on prescription drug terms, and they change. We build campaigns around your program, your physicians and the consultation, and we prepare ad copy with the current policies in mind. Where a certification or approval from the platform is required, we help you through the application.

How do we compete with online companies that charge less?

We do not try to win on price. We present what a local, physician-supervised program includes, such as in-person evaluation, monitoring and a continuing relationship with a physician, so patients who value those things can find and choose you.

Who writes the medical content on our pages and e-mails?

AI assists with drafts, and our writers and physicians review and edit everything before it reaches you. Your own physicians give final approval, so the content reflects how your practice treats patients.

Can you help with patient retention, not only new patients?

Yes. We manage e-mail and text communication for active and lapsed patients, and we connect it to your scheduling or CRM tools where their systems allow. Our reporting shows new enrollments and returning patients separately so you can see both.
