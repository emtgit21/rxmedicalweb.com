What makes this specialty different to market.

We start from how your patients decide, not from a list of services.

  1. Competing on price alone

    When every practice in town advertises a per-unit special, patients learn to shop by discount and leave for the next one. Your marketing has to show who is holding the syringe and why that matters, so you are chosen for trust and not for the lowest offer.

  2. One visit, then silence

    Most of the value of an aesthetic patient comes from the visits after the first, yet many practices spend everything on new leads and nothing on bringing people back. Without reminders, memberships and follow-up, patients drift to whoever shows up in their feed next.

  3. Inquiries that go unanswered

    Aesthetic patients message at night from Instagram, a web form or a text, and they often contact several practices at once. If your front desk replies the next afternoon, the consultation has usually been booked somewhere else.

Advertising with care

  • Before-and-after photos and patient testimonials generally call for written patient consent and an honest presentation of typical results, and we build campaigns with these in mind while your practice confirms the specifics with its counsel.
  • Advertising that names prescription products such as neuromodulators, or that makes claims about results, is subject to federal, state and platform rules, so we write conservatively and the practice confirms the wording with its counsel or compliance contact.
  • Replies to reviews and social comments should never confirm that someone is a patient or mention their treatment, and we draft every reply with patient privacy and HIPAA requirements in mind.

Questions we hear from med spas and aesthetics

Do you work with med spas that are not owned by a physician?

Yes. We work with physician-owned practices and with med spas that operate under a medical director. Because we are physician owned ourselves, we understand how supervision and credentials should be presented, and we ask your medical director to review clinical content.

We already post on Instagram ourselves. What would you add?

Most practices post well but have no path from a post to a booked consultation. We add planning, video of your providers, paid campaigns where they make sense, and a system that captures and answers each inquiry. You can keep posting day-to-day moments while we handle the structure around them.

Who writes our treatment pages and posts?

AI helps us draft and organize content, and our in-house team edits it. Anything medical is reviewed by a physician before it is published, and nothing goes live without your approval.

Can you help us launch a treatment we just trained on?

Yes. As the strategic marketing partner of Empire Medical Training, we often work with physicians who are adding a procedure to their practice. Our new service launch covers the treatment page, announcement to your existing patients, and campaigns to bring in the first bookings.
