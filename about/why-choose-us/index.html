Why choose us?

A written guarantee, fair pricing and a team that understands medicine.

Our guarantee

We offer a full, 100% money back guarantee in writing if you are not satisfied with your website. If you are not happy with your website within the first 15 days after your initial deposit, you will be refunded your entire advance payment. We are confident you will love our work.

Our pricing

Our pricing is reasonable and in most cases more cost effective because of the efficiency of our work. Be careful with website firms whose prices are unbelievably low and whose promises are unrealistic. A refund policy protects you somewhat, but it cannot give back the time you lost. When you choose a web firm, shop around, compare prices, quality and staff, review their portfolio and make sure there is a written refund policy.

Our experience

No matter how much traffic your website gets, the end result should always be a phone call to your practice from a patient interested in your services. Our results and phone tracking system lets you track that. Our clients only pay our fees when we achieve first page results for them.

Our team

  • Family owned. We are a family owned business, dedicated to the best results and service for our clients.
  • In house. We are a medium sized firm in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. All of our work is performed in house.
  • The internet team. Website designers, programmers, graphic designers, videographers, and internet marketing and search engine optimization specialists.
  • The business team. MBAs and marketing experts, plus a physician and legal advisory board for our medical websites.
  • AI at work. We use the latest AI tools to research, draft, test and report faster, with people reviewing the result.

We are unique

We genuinely care about our clients and want you to be successful. We do more than build websites and campaigns. We focus on results you can notice in your practice.

Our MBAs take the time to study your specialty and your competitors, in order to build a marketing plan that is cost effective and aimed at results. Our physician advisory board is made up of physicians from different specialties who provide medical material for your website and marketing that is compelling and medically accurate.

We give our clients personal service and attention, and we take direction only from you.
