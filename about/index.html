The details

Rx Medical Web is a family owned marketing agency dedicated to one thing: the online presence and marketing of medical practices. Since 2008 we have developed marketing and practice building strategies for more than 400 physicians and dentists of all specialties.

Our staff includes website designers and programmers, search engine optimization specialists, physicians and business personnel. All of our people work on site in Fort Lauderdale. We do not outsource.

Part of our success comes from understanding our clients. We understand medical marketing and the dynamics of a medical practice. In addition to our internet staff, we maintain a physician advisory board of specialists who help create and market our clients' websites. Our physician staff writes and reviews website content and marketing emails, and is available for support and consultation.
